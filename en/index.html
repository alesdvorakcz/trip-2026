VietnamWe're here now

Hanoi

6–14 October

Aleš flies ahead and lands in Hanoi on 6 October. The first five days are mostly work, getting things ready for the trip and fighting jet lag, maybe with a visit to one of the craft villages. SanSan and Adéla arrive on the evening of Sunday 11 October. Then we have three days to settle in: we'll meet friends, go out to eat and explore interesting corners of Hanoi. On Wednesday evening we head for the mountains.

Cao Bằng

14–24 October

Karst hills, the green Quây Sơn river, fields after the harvest and villages of the Tày and Nùng peoples. We go there on a night bus with a double cabin and get around by scooter, riding only a short way each day so Adéla has time for a nap and for the animals. The rice will probably be harvested by then, so we expect stubble, straw and buffalo in the fields.

Hanoi, Tây Hồ

24 October – 3 November

Ten days in a flat in Tây Hồ by West Lake, the largest lake in Hanoi. It's a quiet neighbourhood where many expats live. Aleš works, and Adéla has play spaces and playgrounds nearby. In our free time we'll explore Hanoi, try local specialities and take short trips.

Ninh Bình

3–8 November

By train south into a landscape of limestone cliffs, rivers and rice fields. We'll spend part of the stay near Tam Cốc, close to the animal farms, and part in Ninh Bình's old quarter; the order is still to be decided. At the weekend we have dinner with Mai Anh and Minh's parents in Tam Điệp, and on the afternoon of Sunday 8 November we take the train back to Hanoi.

Koh Lanta

11 November – 1 December

Almost three weeks on an island in the Andaman Sea: beaches, sandcastles, snorkelling and diving, sunsets and scooter trips. After landing we spend a night in Krabi Town and stop at the emerald pools in the jungle on the way to the island. On Lanta we stay first in the north by Klong Dao beach, then ten nights at Klong Nin, and finish with a few nights in the quiet south of the island.

We haven't bought the flights yet, so the dates may still shift.

Kaohsiung

2–15 December

On the evening of 1 December we fly from Krabi via Bangkok and land in Kaohsiung on the morning of 2 December. We spend two weeks with SanSan's family, and on 15 December we fly back to the Czech Republic, looking forward to Christmas. We'll send you photos along the way.
